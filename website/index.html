Where Open Sky Meets Unmatched Ambiance

Welcome to Breeze The Rooftop Café — Palghar's ultimate destination for elevated culinary moments. Situated on the breezy 5th floor terrace of Kanchan Aura on Mahim Road, Breeze offers an escape from the city bustle into a scenic open-air oasis.

Whether you are catching the golden sunset over Palghar, celebrating special occasions with friends, or having a cozy romantic dinner under glowing fairy lights, our cafe sets the perfect mood with soul-satisfying flavors and soothing tunes.
