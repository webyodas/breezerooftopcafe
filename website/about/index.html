The Breeze Sanctuary

Where Open Sky Meets Unmatched Ambiance

“A cafe that has the incredible atmosphere and the ambiance is absolutely incredible and totally unmatched.”

Welcome to Breeze The Rooftop Café — Palghar's premier destination for elevated culinary moments. Situated on the breezy 5th floor terrace of Kanchan Aura on Mahim Road, Breeze offers an escape from the city bustle into a scenic open-air oasis.

Whether you are catching the golden sunset over Palghar, celebrating special milestones with friends, or enjoying a cozy candle-lit dinner under glowing fairy lights, our cafe sets the perfect mood with soul-satisfying flavors and soothing tunes.

  • Open Rooftop Terrace with Cozy Cabanas
  • 100% Pure Veg Gourmet Delights & Jain Options
  • Signature Ceremonial Matcha & Cold Brews
  • Warm Acoustic Lighting, Sunset Breezes & Music
