Transparency & Data Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Breeze Rooftop Cafe, Palghar

1. Overview

At Breeze Rooftop Cafe (operated at 5th Floor, Kanchan Aura, Opp. HDFC Bank, Palghar West, Maharashtra 401404), we respect your personal privacy. We ensure that ordering gourmet meals, booking rooftop dining tables, and receiving delivery notifications is completely transparent, seamless, and safe.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information necessary to prepare and deliver your orders:

  • Full Name & Mobile Number: Used to confirm your order, send live tracking status updates, and dispatch riders to your doorstep.
  • Delivery Address & Landmarks: Saved securely on your device and synchronized to our kitchen dispatch system for fast doorstep fulfillment.
  • Order History & Receipts: To allow you to easily view past items, re-order your favorite dishes, and access digital receipts.
  • Table Booking Preferences: Date, time, party size, and celebration notes when reserving a table on our rooftop.

3. Payment Gateway & Financial Security

All digital online payments (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking) are processed directly through Razorpay Payment Gateway, a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant provider. Breeze Rooftop Cafe does not store or process your card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. All transaction data is encrypted with 256-bit SSL encryption.

4. Zero Third-Party Spam & Marketing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. Communications sent to your phone are strictly transactional: live order preparation alerts, delivery tracking links, and reservation confirmations.
