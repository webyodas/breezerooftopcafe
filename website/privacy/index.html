Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Breeze The Roof-Top Cafe, Palghar

1. Overview

At Breeze The Roof-Top Cafe (located at 5th Floor, Kanchan Aura, Above Naturals Ice Cream, Opp. HDFC Bank, Palghar West, Maharashtra 401404), we respect your privacy. This policy outlines how your basic contact information and ordering preferences are handled when you interact with our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information necessary to fulfill your dining, pickup, and delivery requests:

  • Name & Mobile Number: Used to confirm your order, communicate status updates, and coordinate delivery.
  • Delivery Address: Used strictly for doorstep meal delivery and dispatch routing.
  • Order History: Stored locally on your device or linked account to facilitate re-ordering and viewing past receipts.
  • Table Reservation Details: Date, time slot, party size, and seating preferences.

3. Payment Gateway & Financial Security

All digital online payments (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking) are processed directly through certified PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (such as Razorpay). Breeze The Roof-Top Cafe does not store, process, or view your card credentials, CVV, or UPI PINs.

4. Data Protection & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal details to third-party marketing companies. Information is shared only with direct service facilitators (such as delivery riders fulfilling your order) as strictly necessary to deliver your meal.
