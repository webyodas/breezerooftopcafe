Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Breeze The Roof-Top Cafe, Palghar

1. Service Area & Coverage

Breeze The Roof-Top Cafe provides food delivery across Palghar city and immediate surrounding areas from our location at Kanchan Aura, Mahim Road. Delivery availability is subject to address verification at checkout.

2. Preparation & Estimated Delivery Times

All meals are prepared fresh upon order placement. Estimated delivery time typically ranges between 25 and 45 minutes depending on kitchen order load, distance, traffic, and weather conditions. Customers can monitor live kitchen and delivery status via our Live Order Tracking screen.

3. Packaging & Handover

Dishes are packaged in standard food-grade containers to preserve freshness and prevent spillage during transit. Customers are requested to ensure someone is available at the provided delivery address to receive the order promptly upon rider arrival.

4. Delivery Charges

Any applicable delivery fee is shown transparently in the order summary before order placement and payment authorization.
