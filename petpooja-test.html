Petpooja Onboarding Checklist & Script

2. What You Tell Petpooja Support

"Hello, I am calling on behalf of Breeze The Rooftop Cafe (Palghar). We have built their direct online ordering web application. We need to push website orders directly to their billing terminal using Petpooja Open API (push_order). Can you guide us on the approval process to generate the App Key & API Key for this outlet?"

3. What the Owner Needs to Do

  • The owner logs into dashboard.petpooja.com.
  • Goes to Marketplace / Third-Party Integrations.
  • Approves or generates the API Key & App Key.
  • Or sends 1 WhatsApp line to their Petpooja Account Manager: "Please activate Open API for our developer team Webyodas."

4. The 30-Second Plug-in Step

Once the keys are obtained, simply open js/main.js, paste the PETPOOJA_API_KEY and PETPOOJA_APP_KEY into BREEZE_CONFIG, and commit. Orders instantly auto-print on their KOT printer!
